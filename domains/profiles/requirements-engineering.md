# Requirements Engineering

## Purpose
Traceable requirements, acceptance criteria, assumptions and change control.

## Required questions
- What outcome, users, assets and boundaries are in scope?
- What requirements, invariants, assumptions and dependencies govern the work?
- What are the material failure modes, abuse cases and recovery needs?
- Which profile controls and risk class apply?
- What tests and review evidence demonstrate acceptance?
- Who owns operation, change, incident response and periodic review?

## Minimum expectations
- Trace material requirements and decisions to an owner and rationale.
- Record material risks and unresolved assumptions before high-impact work.
- Test changes in proportion to impact and failure likelihood.
- Do not expose secrets or unnecessary sensitive data in code, logs or evidence.
- Report implementation, results and limitations accurately.

## Profile extensions
The OIS profile adds runtime, state, authorization and production-evidence requirements where relevant. The AI Agents profile adds task scope, tool permission, agent provenance and human approval requirements. Applicability must be assessed per change; a domain entry alone does not prove implementation.
