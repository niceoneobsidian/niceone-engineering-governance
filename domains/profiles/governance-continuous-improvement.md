# Governance and Continuous Improvement

## Purpose
Policies, metrics, exceptions, audit, incidents and learning loops.

## Required questions
- Are controls owned, scoped, versioned and measurable?
- Are exceptions approved, time-limited and reviewed?
- Can material claims be traced to evidence?
- Are incidents, defects and drift feeding corrective action?
- Are review cadence, escalation and closure criteria explicit?

## Minimum expectations
Keep stable identifiers, distinguish policy from implementation, preserve evidence provenance, track corrective actions to closure, and review metrics for coverage and misleading incentives. Do not claim compliance from documentation alone.
