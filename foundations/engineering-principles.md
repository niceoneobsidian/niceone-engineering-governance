# Engineering Principles

1. Evidence over assertion.
2. Least privilege and explicit authorization.
3. Separate planning, permission, execution and verification.
4. Fail closed for protected actions.
5. Prefer small, reversible changes and safe checkpoints.
6. Define contracts and invariants before integration.
7. Test according to risk and failure modes.
8. Protect secrets, sensitive data and supply-chain integrity.
9. Make critical states and failures observable without leaking secrets.
10. Record decisions, exceptions and migrations.
11. Distinguish unknown, blocked, failed, skipped and verified.
12. Learn from defects and incidents without weakening safeguards.
13. Preserve accountable human ownership of material risk.
