# Exception Management

An exception is a temporary, narrow deviation. It cannot waive law, contract or mandatory safety/security duties.

Required fields: unique ID; target; control IDs; rationale; risk and affected assets; compensating controls; residual risk; approver; UTC start/expiry; remediation owner/date; review cadence; closure evidence.

Lifecycle: `Requested → Risk Assessed → Approved/Rejected → Active → Renewed/Expired → Closed`.

Approval precedes deviation. Independent approval is expected for high-risk exceptions where feasible. Expired exceptions are invalid. Renewals need fresh rationale. Emergency actions require prompt recording and retrospective review.

Reject records with missing fields, unknown controls, expired dates, no approval, out-of-scope targets or prohibited actions.
