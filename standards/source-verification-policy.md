# External Standards Source Verification

Prefer official publishers and canonical specifications. Record publisher, title, edition/version, publication/status date where known, official URL, applicability, review date, internal control mapping and uncertainty. Distinguish mandatory standards from voluntary frameworks and community guidance.

Before mapping: verify title, identifier, edition and scope; do not invent clause text; note paywalls or unavailable sources; map only relevant requirements; recheck mutable references periodically. A citation is not proof of implementation or conformity. Never claim certification from a partial mapping.

Statuses: `verified_source`, `candidate`, `superseded`, `withdrawn`, `unavailable_to_verify`.
